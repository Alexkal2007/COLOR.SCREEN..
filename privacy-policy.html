Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This privacy policy is a general, informational text. It does not constitute legal advice. If you operate this site commercially, it's recommended that a lawyer review and adapt it according to your jurisdiction and applicable regulations (e.g. GDPR).

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the ColorScreen team ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects any information when you visit or use the website and the tools it offers.

2. What data is collected

The core tool (screen color display, image download, animated screens) runs entirely locally in your browser. No sign-up is required, and no personal details such as name, email, or phone number are requested to use the tool. However, some basic technical data may be automatically collected, such as:

  • Browser type and version
  • Device operating system
  • Screen resolution
  • General country-level location (via IP address), not exact location
  • Pages visited and time spent

3. How the data is used

Any technical data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Understand how the site is used, so we can improve functionality
  • Identify technical issues or bugs
  • Statistical analysis of traffic in aggregated, anonymous form

We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Cookies

The site may use cookies for basic functionality and, if enabled in the future, for analytics. See our Cookie Policy for more details.

5. Storage of images you create

The color images you create via the "Download image" feature are generated locally in your browser (using HTML canvas) and sent directly to your device. We do not store a copy of these images on our servers.

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any data that may be collected. However, no method of data transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing of any personal data concerning you. For questions about your rights, contact us via the "About Us" page.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
