About Us

Last updated: July 2026

ColorScreen started as a simple, free tool that solves one small but common problem: needing a full-screen color in seconds — without opening an image editor, downloading an app, or creating an account.

Our mission

We believe small, everyday tools should be fast, free, and accessible to everyone, on any device. ColorScreen was built around that idea: open instantly, work without ad clutter getting in the way, and require no sign-up at all.

What we offer

The platform offers fullscreen display in various colors, the ability to download a color image at a specific resolution, as well as a range of fun animated screens (screensavers/effects) such as radar, hacker terminal, generic-style boot screens, sports screens, and classic screensavers like the bouncing DVD logo.

Who we are

We're a small, independent team building simple browser tools. We are not affiliated, sponsored, or partnered with any software company, operating system maker, or sports organization that may be loosely referenced in our animated screens. Any such visual element is created by us with our own code and design.

Contact

For questions, improvement suggestions, or to report an issue, you can reach us through the contact form that will be added to this page soon. We appreciate any feedback that helps us make the tool better.
