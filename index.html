Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly does this tool do?

It turns your device's screen into a solid color surface or one of the animated effect screens (broken screen, radar, hacker terminal, etc.) in fullscreen. Useful for lighting during video calls, screen testing, photography, or just for fun.

Do I need to install anything or sign up?

No. The tool runs entirely in your browser. No account is needed, no app download, and no payment is required.

Does it work on mobile and tablet?

Yes, the page is fully responsive and works in mobile and tablet browsers. On some mobile browsers, fullscreen behavior may vary slightly due to OS limitations.

Can a full-color screen damage my display?

No, displaying a solid color on your screen causes no hardware damage whatsoever. It's often used for the opposite reason: to spot dead pixels or uneven backlighting.

How do I download a color image?

Pick the color you want, choose a resolution (or enter your own dimensions), and click "Download image." A PNG file will download automatically to your computer or phone.

Do you collect my personal data?

We don't require sign-up and don't collect personal information for using the core tool. For details on any cookies or usage analytics, see our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.

Are the animated screens (Windows, Mac, Ubuntu, etc.) official?

No. These are generic, inspired visual recreations built with our own code, style, and colors. We don't use any official logos or registered trademarks of third-party companies, and we're not affiliated with them.

Can I use the tool on a TV or big screen?

Yes, as long as your device has a browser. Open the page, pick the color or effect you want, and hit fullscreen.
